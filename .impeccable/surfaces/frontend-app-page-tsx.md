Memorable moment: the live map with 10,000 customers on real streets.

## Direction contract

THESIS: America already mapped every block for one question: is this building a good risk? StrataIQ is that atlas redrawn for your address. It refuses the SaaS hero with a floating dashboard, and it refuses dark dashboards with glowing charts.

OWN-WORLD: Deep atlas blue #1d4f91 as the committed ground. Cool white map sheets #f7f8fa. Flat brick pink #e98f86 = measured. Frame yellow #f2c94c = yours (your site, your entries, your next step). Pale stone blue = estimated. Ink #0f1f3d outlines. Flat fills with a 1px ink outline, square corners, no shadows, no gradients as surface. Sheet numbers in corners, a printed color key, scale bars.

STORY: The visitor sees their kind of block as an atlas sheet, understands that customers are simulated on real streets, believes it because every number wears its color, and tests an address.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed 3D block in atlas colors on blue with moving customer dots. Lower left, a white map sheet carries the headline, one sentence, address box and yellow button. Lower right, the live color key. Sheet number top right, wordmark top left.

FORM: Sanborn fire insurance atlas, number 4 on the ordered list. seed:d10cb0c5d0a00411. Raised from Night Instruments: six cross-checking numbers in a fixed place on the verdict. Raised from Setup Wizard: always exactly one clear next step.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
